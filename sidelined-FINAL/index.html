Before you start

This is not medical advice, and it is not a diagnosis. Sidelined is a general-information tool. It can be wrong. It is not a substitute for a qualified clinician.

If something feels seriously wrong — numbness, a joint that looks out of place, a loud pop followed by instability, or you're generally unwell — don't wait on an app. Call 111 (NHS non-emergency) or 999 in an emergency.
